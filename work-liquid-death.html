Problem

Valentine’s Day pressures couples to prove their love through spending.

Insight

Good relationships already require healthy sacrifice: your solo weeknight routines, your chance to pick the movie tonight, or maybe even your side of the bed. It’s all been murdered. And honestly? It’s so worth it.

Idea

This Valentine’s Day, we built an online cemetery for everything love has “killed.” Give those sacrifices the send-off they deserve, because the greatest gift this Valentine’s is an all-in commitment. Don’t buy love. Kill for it.
